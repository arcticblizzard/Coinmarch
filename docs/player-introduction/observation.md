# Observation

Perception and observation are incredibly important tools in any TTRPG. In the _Warlock!_ system, the only skill conceivably tied to perception (as found in D&D) is the __Spot__ skill.

As access to this skill is somewhat limited and there is no passive perception outlined in the core mechanics, I have instituted the following mechanic to help guide character investigation of environments.

Much of these mechanics are tied closely to a character creation item: Expertises. More on that feature can be found [here](/Coinmarch/player-introduction/character-creation/expertise/).

Tuning into ones surroundings to find information and clues has been broken out into three distinct layers:

| Perception Layer | Roll Required? | Player Requirement | Information Provided |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General Perception | No | Nothing | General layout of an environment and its contents (provided character is present and able to perceive their surroundings) |
| Observation | No | Player provides general things they are observing in the environment that may be tied to an Expertise | Specific details associated to a chosen Expertise that may reveal additional insights, information, or clues. |
| Investigation | Yes, Spot | Player provides specific items they are investigating in the environment*. | Upon successful Check, GM will provide any insights, information, or clues associated with the inspected item. |

_* If the investigation involves the use of an Expertise, the Player will recieve a +2 to their Spot Check._

Below is an example of play with this mechanic:
>GM: “You step into the council chamber. Marble dais ahead, cracked stained‐glass windows, and a bronze brazier off to one side. Is there anything you'd like to focus on or generally observe?”

>Leora (Expertise: Mysterium): “I’m observing the stained glass for arcane symbols.”

>GM (free clue): “You spot a faint shimmer around that blue pane—as if a ward once held back something.”

>Leora: “I’ll Spot the frame for a hidden release mechanism.”

>Roll: Leora’s Spot + 2 vs. 20 → on success, she finds a tiny catch beneath the sill. On a fail, she may still find it with an expenditure of time or some other setback occuring.
