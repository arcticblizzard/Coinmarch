# Expertise

Your characters within the game will have a number of interests that may or may not relate to their chosen vocation. These interests will directly tie to observation and perception mechanics in the game. The examples in the list below are not exhaustive, but should serve as a guidepost to the types of specific areas your character may have knowledge on.

Please choose two Expertises from the following list:

| Expertise | Related Topics |
| --- | --- |
| Arts & Design | Architecture, Arts, Performance |
| Bibliophile | Books,Foreign Tongues, Archaic Scripts, Slang |
| Ciphers | Mathmatics, Codes, Inscriptions |
| Crafting | Crafts, Metallurgy, Tools |
| Custom | Diplomacy, Heraldry |
| Engineering | Mechanics, Construction |
| Geography | Cartography, Navigation, Currents, Weather |
| Gourmet | Cooking, Ingredients, Potent Potables |
